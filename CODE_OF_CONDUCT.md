# Code of conduct

## The standard

Everyone who takes part here gets a harassment-free experience, whatever their
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

Behaviour that helps:

- Show empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect a different opinion, viewpoint, or experience.
- Give useful feedback, and accept it with grace.
- Take responsibility, apologise to the people affected by a mistake, and learn.
- Put the good of the whole community before your own.

Behaviour that does not belong here:

- Sexual language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or email
  address, without their explicit permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would call unprofessional.

## This project argues about numbers, not people

The whole point of this repository is that a published figure can be checked.
Challenge a number, a method, or an assumption as hard as you like. Bring the
record you compared it against. That is the work.

Do not turn a disagreement about a figure into a judgement about the person who
published it.

## Scope

This applies in every project space. It applies in any other space when a person
speaks there for the project.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer
through GitHub. Every report gets a review, and the reporter's privacy and
security are respected.
