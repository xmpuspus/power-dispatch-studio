# Three sets of terms, by what the file is

`LICENSE` holds the MIT text on its own, because GitHub matches that file
against the exact template and shows "Other" when anything is appended to it.
The scope note lives here instead.

## The code is MIT

`pipeline/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, `tools/`, `build/`, `src/power_dispatch/`,
`studio/src/`, `web/*.html`, `web/serve.py` and the `Makefile`.

## The calculated data is CC BY 4.0

`web/data/*.json` and `web/data/*.geojson` carry
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) as well as MIT. Take
whichever one fits your use.

Attribution for a redistribution:

> Power Dispatch Studio (2026), IEMOP public market data archive,
> https://github.com/xmpuspus/power-dispatch-studio

## The mirrored market files stay with their publishers

`data/raw/` holds public files from the Independent Electricity Market Operator
of the Philippines (IEMOP) and other publishers, republished as-is for research
with attribution. The publishers keep their own terms. Send a takedown request
through the repository issues and it gets honored.
